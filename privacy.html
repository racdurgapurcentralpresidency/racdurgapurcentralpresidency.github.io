Privacy Policy & Analytics Notice

Last Updated: November 2026 • Rotaract Club of Durgapur Central Presidency

1 About RAC DCP

RAC DCP is the Rotaract Club of Durgapur Central Presidency. This page explains the website's visitor analytics and general digital privacy practices.

2 Website Analytics

RAC DCP uses Umami Cloud to understand aggregate website usage, including page visits, referral sources, device information and approximate geographic location. This information helps us evaluate website usage and improve access to our public resources.

✓ Cookie-less Architecture ✓ URL Query & Hash Excluded ✓ Do Not Track (DNT) Respected ✓ EU Data Region Storage

Our analytics integration is configured without analytics cookies, excludes URL query parameters and fragments, and respects the browser's Do Not Track (DNT) preference where supported.

Umami processes technical connection information, including IP addresses and browser information, to generate visitor statistics and approximate geographic information. IP addresses are not included in standard Umami analytics records. However, we do not make claims about the absence of IP addresses from all operational or security logs maintained by the provider or its infrastructure partners.

The analytics integration does not intentionally include CARE CONNECT application details in analytics events. Applicant names, phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses, application reference numbers and form contents are not used as analytics event data.

Our Umami Cloud website is configured in the European Union (EU) data region for analytics storage. This does not necessarily mean that every supporting technical or operational process takes place exclusively within the EU.

For further information about the provider's data practices, please read the Umami Privacy Policy.

3 CARE CONNECT and Community Assistance

CARE CONNECT collects information submitted by individuals and organisations when they request community assistance or propose collaboration. Such information is handled separately from website visitor analytics and is used for the relevant assistance, verification and communication purposes described in the CARE CONNECT Application Privacy Policy.

Visitors wishing to review application-specific data practices may consult the CARE CONNECT Application Privacy Policy directly on the platform.

## 8. Data Limitations and Interpretation

To ensure strict factual accuracy, the following boundaries govern the interpretation of this baseline:

1. **Aggregate Position Caveat:** The average position of 4.0 is an aggregate metric across the 8 recorded impressions. It does not represent a confirmed ranking of position 4 for any specific search keyword or brand query.
2. **Geographic Boundaries:** Reported geography is strictly national (India). These figures provide no empirical evidence regarding local visibility within Durgapur, Paschim Bardhaman, Kolkata, Naihati, or West Bengal.
3. **Query Omission:** Because no query rows are present in the export, specific user search terms cannot be identified or inferred.
4. **Search Engine Scope:** This report reflects Google Web Search traffic exclusively. It does not track non-Google search engines (Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo) or direct navigation.
5. **Absence of On-Site Engagement Data:** Google Search Console measures SERP-level impressions and clicks only. It provides no information regarding user sessions, bounce rates, time on page, interactive gallery usage, or CARE CONNECT assistance form submissions.
6. **No Inferred Statistics:** All values in this report derive strictly from the supplied GSC export without interpolation or unsupported claims.

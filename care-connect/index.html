Clear & Transparent

How CARE CONNECT Works

A clear, 4-step process designed to ensure fair verification, structured planning, and transparent community delivery.

1

Tell Us About Your Requirement

Fill out our structured multi-step form with your contact information, exact requirements, location, and relevant details.

2

Submit & Receive Reference ID

Once submitted, your request is logged and you receive a unique Care Connect Reference ID (e.g. CCD-2026-0001) for future reference.

3

Our Team Reviews & Verifies

The RAC DCP Community Service Directorate reviews the submission, verifies authenticity, and assesses feasibility and available resources.

4

We Connect & Coordinate

If feasible, our directors contact you via phone or WhatsApp to coordinate direct assistance or structure an impactful partnership.

Privacy Policy • CARE CONNECT

Last Updated: November 2026

The Rotaract Club of Durgapur Central Presidency (RI District 3240) values your personal privacy. This platform is operated purely for non-commercial community assistance and collaboration.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the contact information (such as name, phone number, email address, locality) and requirement descriptions willingly submitted by you via this form. We never request or store sensitive financial passwords, bank account details, or government identity PINs.

2. Purpose of Collection

All data collected is used strictly by our authorized club leadership to review requests, verify community needs, and contact you regarding possible assistance or collaboration.

3. Non-Disclosure & Zero Monetization

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any commercial third party or advertisers. Data is accessible solely to authorized members of the Board of Directors of RAC DCP.

4. Data Retention

Submission records are maintained for institutional audit, reporting to Rotary International District 3240, and verifying historical aid delivery.

Terms of Service • CARE CONNECT

1. Voluntary Platform

RAC DCP CARE CONNECT is a voluntary humanitarian bridge. Submission of a request or collaboration proposal does not constitute a legal contract, financial commitment, or guarantee of assistance from the Rotaract Club of Durgapur Central Presidency or Rotary International.

2. Truthful Disclosures

Applicants agree to provide authentic and truthful information. Submitting fraudulent claims, impersonating individuals, or deploying automated spam will lead to immediate blacklisting and reporting to appropriate civil authorities.

3. Emergency Limitation

This platform does not handle acute emergency services. Please contact local hospitals, fire, or police services directly for emergency assistance.

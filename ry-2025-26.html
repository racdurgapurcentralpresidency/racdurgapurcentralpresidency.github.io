Community Service #05 📸 5 Photos
📅 2nd August 2025
📍 Universal Smile Children’s Home, Anandapur, WB - 700107

Boier Desh Universal Smile Day 1

On 2nd August 2025, we visited Universal Smile Orphanage situated at Anandapur, Kolkata 700107 to conduct a survey. We wanted to know the children's requirements and necessities before conducting our project Boier Desh. We have been conducting this since last year and every year we adapt and change according to the children’s requirements. A total of 31 students were present that day at the orphanage, out of 42 students, some absent due to tuition. Before starting our survey, we distributed Bourbon biscuits to all students, so they can have a snack while we work and interact with them. We started chatting with each student one by one, got to know their names, their passion and aim for the future. Each answer were quite unique and we identified what were their requirements and where they need our help. We will be conducting classes every Tuesday and Saturday from now on.

District Project Re-Flag

On 12th August 2025, our Secretary Rtr. Debarpito Sarkar started making Indian flags for this project. He used old A4 pages which were used for previous Club Bulletin spare printouts. On the same time, our Vice-President Rtr. Pratisruti Dutta started arrangements for a flag hosting event at our Boier Desh venue at Ganga Brick Factory, Kankinara. She along with our club volunteer Bhromor Nath visited the office of the BDO and SDO of Barrackpore, to invite them at the venue as Chief Guests. She also coordinated with local volunteers to help on the preparations of the event.

On 14th August 2025, Rtr. Debarpito went to Kankinara, gave his handmade 25 flags to Rtr. Pratisruti. On 15th August 2025, Rtr. Pratisruti joined by our Club Editor Rtr. Monimoy Ghosh & volunteer Bhromor, went to the event venue. The BDO of Barrackpore Block-1 - Mrs. Gargi Das, came to the venue as the Chief Guest. More than 50 children and their parents were also present at the venue.

The BDO hoisted the flag, followed by the children singing the National Anthem. Rtr. Monimoy helped distribute pens and diaries brought by him, and the brick factory manager distributed Cadbury chocolates. The highest point from the event was the BDO recognizing the children's hunger for education and our efforts, thus sponsoring students to attend a tuition program locally for exam prep and higher study prep free of cost.

Boier Desh Universal Smile Day 2

On 19th August 2025, we visited Universal Smile Children's home at Anandapur Kolkata for our 2nd class. That day we organized a free English class for students studying between class VIII to XII. We interacted with them and identified their weaknesses and strengths. We focused on strengthening their reading & writing skills, their grammar skills, speaking in English, communication in English. EDU BRIDGE: Boier Desh

As part of the District Project EDU BRIDGE: Boier Desh, we conducted educational sessions on Spoken English and Grammar at Universal Smile Children’s Home, Kolkata. The initiative aimed to enhance the communication skills and linguistic proficiency of children residing in the institution, thereby supporting their overall academic and personal development. The classes were designed to introduce students to the fundamentals of English grammar, sentence formation, and conversational fluency. The focus was not only on improving their language skills but also on building confidence and encouraging self-expression. Through this initiative, the club sought to bridge educational gaps by bringing structured learning opportunities to children who have limited access to such resources. The project reinforced the importance of language education as a tool for empowerment and lifelong growth.

District Project #14 📸 5 Photos
📅 5th October 2025
📍 Rotary Club of Durgapur, Durgapur, WB - 713214

EDU BRIDGE: Rotaract Literacy Conclave

As part of the District Project EDU BRIDGE: Rotaract Literacy Conclave, the Rotaract Club of Durgapur Central Presidency, in collaboration with the Rotaract Club of Durgapur Central, organized a literacy conclave on the theme “Early Education Against Addiction: Peer Pressure and Substance Abuse – Navigating Teen Challenges.” The initiative aimed to raise awareness among students and young adults about the challenges of peer pressure, the dangers of substance abuse, and the importance of early preventive education.

The conclave featured two distinguished speakers — Mr. Aditya Kumar Sinha, who retired as Assistant General Manager from Durgapur Steel Plant in October 2022 and had previously served as a teacher for fifteen years, and Rtn. Subrata Chattopadhyay, an esteemed Rotarian and educationist. Drawing from his dual experience in education and industry, Mr. Sinha shared valuable insights on how early moral and social education can guide young minds away from harmful influences and towards purposeful living. Rtn. Subrata Chattopadhyay delivered an informative session accompanied by a PowerPoint presentation, highlighting the psychological, social, and health implications of drug abuse. His session emphasized the role of families, schools, and community organizations in fostering awareness and resilience among youth.

The conclave witnessed active participation from members of the Rotaract Club of DSMS College and NSHM Knowledge Campus Durgapur, along with several students, educators, and professionals from the region. The event successfully created an engaging platform for dialogue, learning, and community collaboration on one of the most pressing social issues affecting youth today.

Anti-Addiction Education Joint Conclave DSP & Rotary Speakers

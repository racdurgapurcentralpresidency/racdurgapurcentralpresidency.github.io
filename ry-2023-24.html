Community Service #01 📸 3 Photos
📅 11th October 2023
📍 Prince Anwar Shah Road, Tollygunge, Kolkata 700032

Hand Me Down

On 12th October 2023, our club organized a donation drive of 'Hand Me Down' clothes at Tollygunge. This was our first event as a Rotaract Club for the Rotary Year 2023-24. We distributed 120 different sets of clothes, including T-shirts, Full pants, Jeans, Kurtas, Sarees and kids wear to 28 people living near Navina Cinema Hall, Prince Anwar Shah Road, Tollygunge.

First Club Event Clothes Donation Tollygunge
Community Service #02 📸 1 Photo
📅 14th October 2023
📍 Prince Anwar Shah Road, Tollygunge, Kolkata 700032

Project Zero

Project Zero is an initiative taken by young rotaractors to become action takers and solve the problems of underprivileged people. At Project Zero, we aim to conduct reconnaissance for problem statements such as nutrition, sanitation and education of the underprivileged followed by implementation of sustainable projects that solves the assessed problems.

Flagship Initiative Reconnaissance & Survey Sustainable Impact
Community Service #03 📸 5 Photos
📅 14th October 2023 to 20th April 2024
📍 Ramchandrapur Primary School, Kolkata 700153

Boier Desh

Boier Desh is an initiative taken by us to establish a well-equipped library at a local school at Ramchandrapur, a panchayat area in Kolkata. We recognized the importance of fostering a conducive learning environment and providing students with access to diverse resources to support their academic growth. We conducted English, Mathematics, Aptitude, Literature and Drawing classes to supplement the school curriculum and enhance students' proficiency in these subjects.

UGRAM Award Winner Library Setup Primary Education
Community Service #04 📸 1 Photo
📅 14th October 2023
📍 81, Nilgunj Rd, Agarpara, Kolkata 700109

Alor Benu

On the auspicious day of Durga Puja, our club along with Helping Hut NGO conducted a clothes donation drive called "Alor Benu" at a slum near JIS University, Sodepur. All clothes were newly purchased from the market and distributed to children in between age of 2 to 14 years. We hoped that on the arrival of Maa Durga, we can share happiness and joy with everyone, overcoming any obstacles.

Durga Puja Drive New Clothes Gift Helping Hut NGO
Community Service #05 📸 1 Photo
📅 15th October 2023
📍 Agarpara, Barrackpore, Rabindra Sarovar, Tollygunge

Utthaan Abhiyaan

On October 15th, our club members in collaboration with the Helping Hut NGO team in Kolkata embarked on the 'Utthaan Abhiyaan'. Our dedicated rotaractors ventured into the heart of Barrackpore, Agarpara, Tollygunge and Rabindra Sarovar Lake regions. We stood beside the financially deprived, shedding light on the myriad benefits and privileges that the Government of India offers through its diverse schemes.

Govt Scheme Awareness Civic Rights Helping Hut NGO

Panno Ki Duniya

With hearts full of compassion, our club along with Helping Hut recently visited New Life Society Orphanage, creating cherished memories with 30 wonderful children. Orphanage Visit Mini Library Drawing Competition

Christmas Chronicles

For each and every young one, Christmas is the time for merriment and joy, with thoughtful gifts from loved ones. Keeping the same in mind, our club in association with Helping Hut NGO Kolkata and Autism Society West Bengal, organized Christmas Chronicles, a Sit and Draw Event and Christmas Party for the children from the Autism Society West Bengal. There were gifts, laughter, and a lot of fun activities so that the children feel the spirit of Christmas along with us.

Autism Society WB Sit and Draw Christmas Party
